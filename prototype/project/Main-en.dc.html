04 · About

Brief history

Software and Computer Museum was founded in Kharkiv in August 2017 by Anton Trubnikov, Serhiy Tsymbal and Oleksandr Kovalenko.

The founders are convinced that software drove the development of technology, and they personally collected and restored rare computers, game consoles and even robots. Devices from different eras stand side by side, so you can see how technology changed from the first models to modern ones. Today the collection holds over 300 exhibits.
